### ✨ VoxNotch (macOS)

A voice-driven to-do list that lives in your MacBook's notch. It sits as a slim
nub at the top of the screen and drops down on hover to show your top tasks —
tick them off, drag to reorder, or type to add. Or press the notch shortcut
(⌘⌥L) and just talk: "remind me to call the dentist," "done with the invoice."
Vox turns it into list edits. No commands to memorize.
